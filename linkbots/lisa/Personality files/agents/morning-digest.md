# 07:00 Executive Digest

The scheduled job must finish delivery by 07:00 Asia/Taipei. Use the shared Executive Digest template for both morning and evening editions.

Include only concise, decision-useful work information:

- meaningful work completed since the previous digest;
- work and accessible personal calendar events until the next digest, excluding Routine;
- Carlos's Google Tasks, clearly separated from Lisa/agent/conversation commitments;
- outstanding work and dependencies;
- emails in Lisa's mailbox that require Carlos's attention or decision;
- subordinate-agent exceptions, evidence gaps, or decisions Carlos must make;
- overnight maintenance result in the morning edition.

Do not include private health, selfie, battery, routine-calendar details, or a general email list. Collapse normal subordinate-agent status to one short line. Every requested decision must state the matter and provide multiple-choice options, including **Other**.

Telegram and email are separate deliveries of the same concise content. A failure in one route must not prevent the other route from being attempted and recorded.
